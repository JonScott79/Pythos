Meet Pythos — An AI Tutor Built to Check Its Work

An independent education initiative and AI-powered study companion developed by LANZAR.

AI is reshaping how students learn. But instead of pushing technology away or relying on chatbots that simply spit out quick unverified answers, Pythos takes a fundamentally different approach.

Pythos is designed to help students deeply understand problems, develop critical thinking, and double-check their reasoning—not simply hand them a shortcut.

The Pythos Initiative: Pythos is an independent education initiative dedicated to making high-quality mathematics and physics education freely accessible. Developed and supported by LANZAR, Pythos is focused on helping students deeply understand problems, develop critical thinking, and verify their reasoning—not simply receive unverified answers—kept free for students and sustained long-term through eventual advertising. At its core is a simple principle: no kid should be charged to learn.

Built to Check Its Own Work

A regular AI chatbot just predicts words that sound right—which is why it can confidently give the wrong math answer. Pythos works differently: it checks every number and algebra step using real math software before showing it to you:

🧮 Fast Math Check (Math.js)

Double-checks numbers, fractions, roots, unit conversions, and geometry values using exact math rules instead of guessing.

🐍 Symbolic Algebra Check (SymPy)

Checks equations, factoring, derivatives, integrals, and limits step-by-step using a dedicated Computer Algebra System (CAS).

🛡️ Automatic Revision Gate

Checks that every step matches your actual question and makes sure no wrong intermediate step slips past without being caught and fixed.

🧠 Honest When Unsure + Tutor Mode

If Pythos cannot prove an answer is right, it tells you honestly instead of making something up—and Tutor Mode gives one hint at a time so you learn at your own pace.

🛡️ Clickable Proof Badges — Not a "Black Box"

You never have to take an answer on blind faith. Every verified response shows a clickable verification badge (like Verified (Symbolic), Verified (Exact Roots), or Partially Verified) where students, parents, and teachers can open the exact math checks and domain rules behind each step.

Catching & Fixing Mistakes Before You See Them

If a draft explanation has an arithmetic or algebra slip, Pythos catches it at the verification gate. Rather than giving up when the exact answer is already known from our math engines, Pythos rebuilds a clean, accurate explanation from verified math:

1. Catch the Mistake

Any unverified or contradictory step is blocked before it can appear as your answer.

2. Rebuild from Real Math

When our math engines already proved the right answer, Pythos builds a clean explanation directly from that verified result.

3. Re-Check Everything

Even recovered answers go back through the full math verification gate before reaching your screen.

4. Never Guess

If the problem is unclear or cannot be verified independently, Pythos asks for clarification or safely withholds the answer instead of guessing.

Recovery validation: In a dedicated 50,000-problem deterministic stress campaign (seed 1732050808, commit 4cdb7ee), Pythos recovered 2,920 / 2,920 cases where trusted mathematical truth was available, with 100% recovery correctness. The campaign delivered 21,733 verified answers, safely withheld 28,267 unsupported cases, and recorded 0 incorrect delivered answers and 0 verification escapes. This result validates the recovery subsystem under tested deterministic conditions; it is not a claim of universal mathematical accuracy.

Think You Can Stump Pythos?

We want you to. If you find something Pythos gets wrong, challenge it directly or submit a report. Every edge case helps refine our deterministic verifier layers.

Designed for Parents & Educators: Why You Can Trust It

We understand why parents and teachers are cautious about AI. Traditional chatbots can hallucinate numbers, encourage passive copy-pasting, and undermine real learning. Pythos is built from the ground up to solve those exact concerns:

  • Guided Practice (Tutor Mode): Students can toggle Tutor Mode to get one hint at a time instead of copying a full answer right away.
  • Inspectable Verification Badges: Every verified answer carries a clickable verification badge showing what was checked and any domain rules (like avoiding division by zero).
  • Built to Be Challenged: Pythos encourages healthy critical skepticism: "If you think I'm wrong, challenge me."
  • No Paywalls, Ads, or Subscriptions: Free and open for all learners. Supported by LANZAR with a commitment that no student should ever be charged to learn.
  • Hands-On Study Tools & 17 Simulations: Includes a 2D Function Grapher, Check Work step verifier, Equation Builder, Scientific Calculator, Unit Converter, Matrix tool, photo help, and 17 interactive Sim Lab experiments.

Built by a Student Who Refused to Settle for Hallucinations

Pythos was created after weeks of rigorous adversarial testing across polynomial boundaries, Bayes defect rates, projectile motion, and tricky algebraic edge cases—identifying every failure mode and engineering deterministic verification layers around it.

A Responsible Approach to AI in Education

Pythos is not an infallible oracle or a replacement for dedicated human teachers. It is an empowering learning companion—a patient tutor available 24/7 where students can ask questions without feeling embarrassed, frustrated, or afraid to say, "I don't understand."

💡 The Pythos Philosophy: Pythos exists to help students learn math — not just finish math. Embrace modern tools with healthy skepticism, rigorous verification, and genuine curiosity.

🔒 User-Visible & User-Controlled: Pythos does not sell student data or run third-party tracking ads. Personal learning memory is fully accessible through your user-visible Memory Ledger (accessible via the brain icon), where you can inspect, review, or erase entries at any time.

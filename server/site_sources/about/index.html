Meet Pythos — An AI Tutor Built to Check Its Work

An AI-powered math and physics study companion created by Jon Scott within the LANZAR initiative.

AI is reshaping how students learn. But instead of pushing technology away or relying on chatbots that simply spit out quick unverified answers, Pythos takes a fundamentally different approach.

Pythos is designed to help students deeply understand problems, develop critical thinking, and double-check their reasoning—not simply hand them a shortcut.

The LANZAR Initiative: LANZAR is an independent initiative dedicated to education and building technology for future learners and developers. Pythos is a mathematics and physics education project developed within the LANZAR initiative.

Built to Verify, Not Just Answer

Unlike a traditional chatbot that generates a statistical guess and moves on, Pythos is engineered with a strict three-layer verification architecture designed specifically for educational rigor:

🧮 Layer 1: Deterministic Math.js Engine

Evaluates exact arithmetic, roots, fractions, unit conversions, and geometric constraints in-process without relying on generative approximations.

🐍 Layer 2: Deterministic SymPy CAS

Provides deep symbolic computer algebra for calculus (limits, derivatives, integrals), differential equations, and algebraic equivalence.

🛡️ Layer 3: Proprietary Pythos Verifier

Orchestrates claim extraction, prompt-to-claim fidelity, automated revision with re-verification, deterministic supremacy, vision verification of student diagrams, and the delivery decision gate.

🧠 Safe Withholding & Socratic Dialogue

If a mathematical claim cannot be independently established, Pythos safely withholds the answer (returning UNKNOWN) rather than guessing. Students are guided with progressive Socratic hints.

Think You Can Stump Pythos?

We want you to. If you find something Pythos gets wrong, challenge it directly or submit a report. Every edge case helps refine our deterministic verifier layers.

Designed for Parents & Educators: Why You Can Trust It

We understand why parents and teachers are cautious about AI. Traditional chatbots can hallucinate numbers, encourage passive copy-pasting, and undermine real learning. Pythos is built from the ground up to solve those exact concerns:

  • Active Socratic Guidance: If a student gets stuck, Pythos provides progressive scaffolding rather than robbing them of insight.
  • Built to Be Challenged: Pythos encourages healthy critical skepticism: "If you think I'm wrong, challenge me."
  • No Paywalls, Ads, or Subscriptions: Free and open for all learners within the LANZAR initiative.
  • Integrated Visual Tools: 2D function graphing, interactive equation palette, scientific calculation, unit conversion, and diagram analysis are built right in.

Built by a Student Who Refused to Settle for Hallucinations

Pythos was created after weeks of rigorous adversarial testing across polynomial boundaries, Bayes defect rates, projectile motion, and tricky algebraic edge cases—identifying every failure mode and engineering deterministic verification layers around it.

“I'm a college student myself, and I know what it's like to stare at a math problem and think, ‘What the hell am I supposed to do with this?’ When I saw my own daughter struggling with math, I decided to build something that could help. Pythos is for every student who just needs someone to explain it differently until it finally clicks.

Every kid deserves a chance to understand math, regardless of what their family can afford. That's why Pythos will always be free.”

— Jon Scott, Creator of Pythos

A Responsible Approach to AI in Education

Pythos is not an infallible oracle or a replacement for dedicated human teachers. It is an empowering learning companion—a patient tutor available 24/7 where students can ask questions without feeling embarrassed, frustrated, or afraid to say, "I don't understand."

💡 The Pythos Philosophy: Pythos exists to help students learn math — not just finish math. Embrace modern tools with healthy skepticism, rigorous verification, and genuine curiosity.

🔒 User-Visible & User-Controlled: Pythos does not sell student data or run third-party tracking ads. Personal learning memory is fully accessible through your user-visible Memory Ledger (accessible via the brain icon), where you can inspect, review, or erase entries at any time.

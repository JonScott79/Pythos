Meet Pythos — A Math Tutor Built to Check Its Work

An AI-powered math and physics study companion developed by a student for students. A LANZAR initiative.

AI is here, and it is reshaping how students learn. But instead of pushing technology away or relying on chatbots that simply spit out quick answers without understanding, Pythos takes a fundamentally different approach.

Pythos is designed to help students deeply understand problems, develop critical thinking, and double-check their reasoning—not simply hand them a shortcut.

Built to Verify, Not Just Answer

Unlike a traditional chatbot that generates a guess and moves on, Pythos is engineered with multiple safeguards designed specifically for educational rigor:

🔬 Independent CAS Verification

Every mathematical derivation can be checked by deterministic engines—auditing equations, roots, probabilities, and physical vector balances.

🔄 Two-Stage Reasoning Architecture

Separates model identification and trap detection from step-by-step verified derivations, catching arithmetic oversights before delivery.

🛡️ Zero-Hallucination Ground Truth

Pre-flight calculations inject mathematical invariants into the prompt context so complex word problems never suffer from drift.

🧠 Verified Learning & Socratic Dialogue

When students challenge derivations, Pythos verifies proposed corrections independently—reinforcing conceptual understanding over guesswork.

Designed for Parents & Educators: Why You Can Trust It

We understand why parents and teachers are cautious about AI. Traditional chatbots can hallucinate numbers, encourage passive copy-pasting, and undermine real learning. Pythos is built from the ground up to solve those exact concerns:

  • Active Socratic Guidance: If a student gets stuck, Pythos provides progressive hints rather than robbing them of the insight.
  • Built to Be Challenged: Pythos encourages healthy critical skepticism: "If you think I'm wrong, challenge me."
  • No Paywalls, Ads, or Subscriptions: Free and open for all learners under the LANZAR educational ecosystem.
  • Integrated Visual Tools: 2D function graphing, interactive equation palette, scientific calculation, and unit conversion are built right in.

Built by a Student Who Refused to Settle for Hallucinations

Pythos was created after weeks of rigorous adversarial testing across polynomial boundaries, Bayes defect rates, projectile motion, and tricky algebraic edge cases—identifying every failure mode and engineering deterministic verification layers around it.

“I'm a college student myself, and I know what it's like to stare at a math problem and think, ‘What the hell am I supposed to do with this?’ When I saw my own daughter struggling with math, I decided to build something that could help. Pythos is for every student who just needs someone to explain it differently until it finally clicks.

Every kid deserves a chance to understand math, regardless of what their family can afford. That's why Pythos will always be free.”

— Jon Scott, Creator of Pythos

A Responsible Approach to AI in Education

Pythos is not an infallible oracle or a replacement for dedicated human teachers. It is an empowering learning companion—a patient tutor available 24/7 where students can ask questions without feeling embarrassed, frustrated, or afraid to say, "I don't understand."

💡 The Pythos Philosophy: Pythos exists to help students learn math — not just finish math. Embrace modern tools with healthy skepticism, rigorous verification, and genuine curiosity.

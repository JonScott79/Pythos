About Pythos

Developed by a student for students. A LANZAR project.

Why Pythos Exists

Pythos was built to give high school and college students a free, focused workspace to work through mathematics and physics problems, explore concepts intuitively, and receive personalized assistance without paywalls or subscription barriers.

Rather than merely supplying canned answers, Pythos uses adaptive Socratic tutoring. It guides you step-by-step through algebraic derivations, calculus proofs, and kinematics setups—helping you understand why each mathematical step is valid.

Core Capabilities

🏛️ Adaptive Socratic AI

Trained to provide conceptual hints, walk through derivations backward, and adapt to your personal pace.

📈 Interactive Graphing

Instantly visualize parabolas, trigonometric curves, and polynomials with deterministic 2D coordinate plotting.

⌨️ Visual Equation Builder

Construct complex fractions, integrals, roots, and matrices with a visual math palette—no LaTeX syntax required.

✅ Check My Work

Verify candidate solutions and variable substitutions deterministically before turning in homework.

🧮 Matrix & Unit Tooling

Calculate determinants, inverses, vector cross products, and convert between metric, imperial, and SI physical units.

🌍 Polyglot Fluency

Fluent in 100+ languages, allowing students worldwide to learn math and physics in their native tongue.

The LANZAR Umbrella

Pythos is proud to operate as a free educational initiative under the LANZAR ecosystem. Built with clean, accessible standards, Pythos is designed to remain free, private, and accessible to learners everywhere.

💡 Responsible AI Notice: Pythos is an AI tutor and, like any artificial intelligence, can sometimes make errors or encounter calculation nuances. Always check important homework or exam answers, verify derivations, and exercise your own mathematical judgment.

Pythos 1.0.0

August 26, 2026

Added

  • Specialized Problem Classifier & Router (problemClassifier.js): Multi-domain routing supporting ARITHMETIC, ALGEBRA, CALCULUS, PROBABILITY, STATISTICS, PHYSICS, and CONCEPTUAL reasoning.
  • Two-Stage Reasoning Engine: Clear separation between Stage 1 (Situation & Model Identification) and Stage 2 (Exact Mathematical & Physical Derivation).
  • Worksheet OCR Math Normalizer (ocrMathNormalizer.js): Parses stacked ASCII fractions, inline ratios, and labeled problem worksheets into structured LaTeX notation.
  • Pre-Flight Deterministic Ground Truth: Pre-computes exact values (<1ms) for constrained optimization, two-class Bayes defect rates, projectile motion, and population ratios.
  • Concurrency & Cancellation Controller: Safe AbortController lifecycle propagation across client disconnects, semaphores, and local inference workers.
  • Cross-Step Internal Consistency Verifier: Catches intermediate and posterior contradictions (e.g. Bayes $0.06 \times 0.30 = 0.018$, not $0.09$).
  • Dynamic Domain-Aware Wait-State UX: Problem-specific rotating status messages during reasoning phases.

Performance

  • Pure arithmetic, fraction simplification, and linear equation queries short-circuit directly to deterministic solvers in 3–49 ms with 0 AI calls.
  • Pre-flight CAS facts inject mathematical boundary conditions to eliminate hallucinations on complex word problems.

Verification & Testing

  • 100% pass across 14 regression test suites and 216 Python CAS verifier unit tests.

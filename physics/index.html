Conceptual Physics & Three-Layer Verification

Physics is more than plugging numbers into formulas—it is understanding conservation laws, vector decomposition, dimensional relationships, and physical diagrams. Pythos helps you set up free-body diagrams, balance forces, verify units across SI and imperial systems, and inspect problem diagrams with multi-step deterministic checks.

Calculations and derivations are independently validated across three distinct verification layers: Math.js for numerical calculations and unit arithmetic, SymPy for symbolic mechanics and calculus, and the Proprietary Pythos Verifier for physical claim extraction, diagram fidelity, and automated revision.

Core Physics Disciplines

✦ Kinematics & Projectile Motion

Solve constant acceleration problems using: $$v = v_0 + at, \quad \Delta x = v_0 t + \frac{1}{2}at^2, \quad v^2 = v_0^2 + 2a\Delta x$$

✦ Newton's Laws & Dynamics

Apply $\Sigma F = ma$, calculate static/kinetic friction ($f_k = \mu_k F_N$), tension in ropes, and centripetal acceleration ($a_c = \frac{v^2}{r}$).

✦ Work, Energy & Momentum

Compute kinetic energy ($E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$), gravitational potential energy ($E_p = mgh$), elastic collisions, and impulse.

✦ Gravitation & Orbital Mechanics

Explore Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation: $$F_g = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$$ escape velocity, and satellite orbits.

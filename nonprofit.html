PYTHOS FOR EVERY STUDENT

Education should be an opportunity, not a privilege.

Pythos is being built toward a nonprofit future: a free mathematics and physics tutor designed to help students learn, practice, and understand.

Our goal is simple: keep Pythos free and put its future resources back into education.

TRY PYTHOS OUR MISSION

THE MISSION

Every student deserves access to patient, individualized help when they're stuck.

Pythos isn't designed to replace teachers. It's designed to give students another place to ask the question they were afraid to ask, work through a problem one step at a time, and understand why an answer is correct.

✦ Supplemental Learning

A patient, 24/7 learning companion where students can ask questions comfortably at their own pace without fear or embarrassment.

✦ Student Understanding

Focusing on conceptual derivations, step verification, and the reasoning behind each step—not mere answer generation.

✦ Teacher Support

Complementing and reinforcing classroom teachers rather than supplanting human educators.

✦ Universal Accessibility

Eliminating paywalls and commercial subscription barriers so every student has access to high-quality STEM assistance.

WHY NONPROFIT?

Pythos isn't being built to become another subscription-based educational product.

The long-term goal is to establish Pythos as a nonprofit organization so that funding can be reinvested into the mission.

BUILT BY A STUDENT. FOR STUDENTS.

Pythos was built by a student, for students.

Pythos began as a software project built by a Massachusetts community-college student who wanted to explore what an AI tutor could become when the goal wasn't simply generating answers.

The project grew into something bigger: a vision for educational technology that students can actually question, explore, and learn from.

“Every student deserves someone patient in their corner when a difficult concept isn't clicking. Pythos started at a community college study desk, and its heart will always remain focused on helping students understand the 'why' behind mathematics and science.”

— Jon Scott, Creator of Pythos

THE FUTURE WE WANT TO BUILD

We don't just want to teach the next generation how to use technology.

We want to help them build it.

A future Pythos nonprofit could support student developers through mentorship, open-source projects, educational technology initiatives, and opportunities to turn their own ideas into working software.

The goal is bigger than an AI tutor. It is a model for giving students access to tools, knowledge, and opportunities to build their own future.

WHERE PYTHOS IS TODAY

Transparent Direction

Pythos is currently an independent educational technology project.

The nonprofit structure is a future goal, not a claim that the organization already exists as a registered nonprofit.

Today, Pythos is free and publicly available while we work toward building the people, partnerships, and organizational structure needed to make the long-term mission sustainable.
